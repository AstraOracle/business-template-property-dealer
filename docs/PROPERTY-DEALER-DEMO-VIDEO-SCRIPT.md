# Property Dealer Demo Video Script

## Product Context

- Product: premium property dealer lead-generation website
- Price range: `₹25,000 to ₹35,000`
- Target customers:
  - Indian property dealers
  - brokers
  - rental agents
  - small builders
  - local real estate consultants
- Core message:
  - this is not just a website
  - it helps capture and organize leads
  - it brings inquiries directly to WhatsApp
  - it helps the dealer look more premium and professional

---

## Simple Recording Tips

- Video ko laptop screen recording ke saath record karo.
- Voice calm aur confident rakho.
- Zyada fast mat bolo.
- Har major section par 1 to 2 second ka pause rakho.
- Property detail page aur lead flow part ko clearly dikhana sabse important hai.
- Admin demo short rakho, bas itna dikhana hai ki dealer khud update kar sakta hai.
- Price screen dikhate time over-explain mat karo. Confidence ke saath bolo.
